What to expect from structural design in Brazil

Construction in Brazil is governed by the ABNT technical standards, and every structure requires a licensed engineer taking formal responsibility for the design. The structural package typically represents 25% to 35% of total construction cost — which makes the choice of structural engineer one of the highest-leverage decisions of the entire investment.

How Hartto works with international developers

Briefings, design reviews and decision meetings happen directly in English with the firm's principals. The engineering itself follows the same discipline we apply to every Brazilian client: comparative studies of structural solutions before committing, dimensioning verified for safety and service, and execution-grade detailing that local construction teams build from without ambiguity. Design work is remote by nature; we deliver projects across multiple Brazilian states.

The track record

More than 700,000 m² designed and more than 300 projects delivered for Brazilian developers, in reinforced and post-tensioned concrete — including comparative studies that reached up to 34% steel savings. The portfolio shows recent towers with their structural areas and developers.
